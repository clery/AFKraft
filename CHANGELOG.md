# Changelog

## 1.1.0

### Added

- Adventure Journal with a free monthly 20-rank reward track, daily and weekly contracts tied to real WoW activities, and permanent achievements.
- Journal rewards including supplies, equipment enhancements, heirloom recipes, heirloom equipment, and cosmetic company titles. Earned rewards that need a hero or slot choice remain available across monthly resets.
- Chest rewards with animated reveals on Retail and Forever, distinct rare reveals, and reward notifications.
- An optimal-gearing action and crafting power comparisons against equipped gear.

### Improved

- Faster offline catch-up with work spread across frames, repeated fights fast-forwarded, and a progress view while rewards are calculated.
- Return summaries, collection completion celebrations, and clearer Journal milestones and reward previews.
- Clickable priority notifications, persistent menu badges, and guidance when new features unlock.
- Smooth scrolling, compact loot layouts, and more consistent buttons and Journal tabs.

### Fixed

- Restored companion attack animations and priest spell effects on Forever.
- Corrected caster hand effects to appear before spell release.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
